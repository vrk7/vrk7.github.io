A text-controllable 3D animal generation model using 3D Gaussian Splatting. It produces fine-grained geometry and lifelike textures from natural-language prompts via Score Distillation Sampling.

I also integrated and benchmarked state-of-the-art generative models (Shap-E, Stable Diffusion, Zero123) in a unified pipeline, evaluating multi-view consistency and geometric fidelity across 330K-sample datasets.
