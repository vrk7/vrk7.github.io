A production voice agent for a non-English language, designed and shipped end to end.

- **Conversation design.** Conversation flow and system prompt design, guardrails on out-of-scope requests, and escalation paths to human handling.
- **Streaming transport.** Bidirectional streaming over a versioned WebSocket protocol with per-connection state machines, session resumption and cancellation propagation.
- **Barge-in.** Sub-400 ms interruption under continuous audio load.
