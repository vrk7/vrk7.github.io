A differentiable wave-optics simulation framework in PyTorch that joins Fourier-domain propagation with neural reconstruction for end-to-end hardware-software co-optimization.

- **GPU pipelines.** FFT-based convolution, complex-valued tensors, AMP and tiled overlap-add reconstruction.
- **Scale.** Optimized training across SLURM-distributed GPU clusters.
