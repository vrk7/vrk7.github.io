A production, high-throughput B2B platform that processes async inbound events.

- **Reliability.** Idempotent webhook consumers, deduplication, rate limiting, exponential backoff and Redis-backed caching.
- **Data.** A full relational schema with migrations, and automated booking flows.
- **Deployment.** Vercel serverless functions with a swappable strategy-pattern provider architecture.
- **Process.** Built spec-first with autonomous coding agents (Claude Code): writing the spec, running the agent, then reviewing and iterating to production.
